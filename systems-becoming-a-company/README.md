# The Wheel Is Becoming a Company

A separate fifteen-minute animated podcast by Abba Bello Kanwa, based on public-safe paraphrases of his systems and recent work reflections. Eighteen chapters cover learning, the writing loop, production evidence, studio relationships, honest repair, simplification, freedom, and peace.

The design extends The Secret Behind the Success v2: Bricolage Grotesque and Hanken Grotesk, indigo, gold, turquoise and coral, the geometric star pattern, ambient particles, layered Web Audio music, and narration-synchronized visual reveals.

## Playback

Open index.html in a modern browser, then choose Play with voice and sound. Controls include play/pause, previous/next chapter, an eighteen-chapter picker, a seek bar, separate music/voice/subtitle toggles, and light/dark theme. Space toggles playback; arrow keys change chapters. Playback pauses when the tab is hidden.

The complete narrator audio is embedded in the HTML. Audio, timed subtitles, and a transcript are also available from the chapter menu.

## Narration

Uses the original narrator, en-US-AndrewNeural, rendered at rate -5% and pitch -2Hz. The complete program is mastered to fifteen minutes with a small pitch-preserving timing adjustment and balanced toward -16 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP. Speech word events drive both subtitles and scene beats. The Hausa north-star line is shown visually; the English narrator explains its meaning.
